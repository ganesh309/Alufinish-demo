Die cleaning & caustic soda recovery

One integrated system. No hazardous waste.

With an integrated DIE-CLAR & CRYSTALFIX system, all the advantages connected to each individual technology are summed together and provide a total answer to the problems caused by conventional die pickling systems.

  • Fully automatic operations, from the die cleaning sequence to spent caustic soda recovery
  • Highly environmentally friendly process
  • Total enhancement of safety for workers
  • Recovery of aluminium metal
  • Recovery of caustic soda
  • No spent toxic or hazardous waste to be disposed of or treated
