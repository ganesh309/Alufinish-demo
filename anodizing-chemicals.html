Anodizing chemicals

From degreasing to sealing — a complete chemical programme for matt, bright and coloured anodized finishes. Manufactured in Bangalore, supported on your line.

Browse the range Request data sheets
Product portfolio

Ten process steps, 32 products.

Pick a step above to narrow the list. Every product links to a one-click technical data sheet request.

Degreasing

STEP 01

Removes oils, grease and workshop residues from the profile surface so the following etch is uniform.

  • MG 19 PA TDS
  • MG 19 L TDS
  • MG 19 NB4 TDS
  • Tecnoclean SA TDS

Acid etching

STEP 02

Acid-based etching for a fine, uniform matt surface ahead of anodizing.

  • BE 11 L TDS
  • BE 12 TDS

Alkaline etching

STEP 03

Caustic etching removes the natural oxide layer and gives the classic matt aluminium finish.

  • MG 32 TDS
  • MG 39 TDS

Desmutting

STEP 04

Clears the smut of alloying elements left after etching, leaving a clean surface for the anodizing bath.

  • Neutron 187 C TDS
  • Neutron 190 TDS

Anodizing

STEP 05

Products for the anodizing bath, where the protective, colourable oxide layer is built up.

  • WM 80 L TDS
  • AOX 300 TDS

Pair with FREEAL Plus for automatic control of aluminium concentration and pulse rectifiers for faster cycles.

Electrocolouring

STEP 06

Tin-based electrolytic colouring for reproducible shades from light bronze through to black.

  • Tin Sulphate TDS
  • Salmix NF 40 TDS
  • Salmix NF P TDS

Powered by the Tecno 7S static power supply for improved uniformity and throwing power.

Special electrocolouring

STEP 07

Processes and additives for special electrocolouring shades beyond the standard bronze range.

  • Salkorall TDS
  • Warmall 1 & 2 TDS
  • Tecnostab TDS

Looking for grey, blue or green? See the Multicolor process.

Dye colours

STEP 08

Organic dyes for coloured anodized finishes — from yellow and rose gold to deep black.

  • Orfix TDS
  • Yellow AGR TDS
  • Rose Gold 2BR TDS
  • Black WH TDS

Sealing

STEP 09

Sealing closes the pores of the anodic layer for lasting corrosion and stain resistance.

  • Hardwall 3CB1 TDS
  • Pre-Fix Seal TDS
  • Hardwall MTS TDS
  • MG Seal TZ TDS
  • MG 11 TDS
  • Superseal 2S TDS

Sealing balls for the tanks are available under tools.

Special additives

STEP 10

Additives for bath control, stability and process efficiency across the line.

  • MG 160 TDS
  • Stab F63 TDS
  • GR 20 L TDS
  • MG 21 TDS
Finishes

From natural silver to bronze, black — and Multicolor.

Electrocolouring with tin sulphate and the Salmix range covers the classic bronze-to-black palette. Dye colours add yellow, rose gold and black, and the Multicolor process extends the range to grey, blue, green and many other colours.
