Before you write

What helps us answer faster.

For chemicals

Process (anodizing or pre-treatment), the step you want to improve, alloy and current bath parameters.

For plants

Throughput, maximum profile length, available floor space and whether you prefer a vertical, compact or horizontal layout.

For water treatment

Flow rates, discharge limits, and whether you are aiming for partial recycling or zero liquid discharge.
