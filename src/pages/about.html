About us

Italian Expertise. Indian Presence.

Italtecno India Pvt. Ltd., based in Bangalore, India, is a manufacturer and supplier of high-quality chemicals for the aluminium surface treatment and finishing industry.

Combining Italian technological expertise with local knowledge and technical support, we provide specialized chemical solutions, customized plants, and equipment for anodizing, powder coating, extrusion die cleaning, and aluminium finishing processes.

Our commitment to quality, technical excellence, and customer satisfaction enables us to deliver reliable, efficient, and customized solutions that meet the evolving needs of our customers.

Our Solutions

At Italtecno India Pvt. Ltd., we provide comprehensive solutions for the aluminium surface treatment and finishing industry, combining advanced chemical technologies with Italian-engineered plants and equipment.
