Italtecno India Pvt. Ltd. is a Bangalore-based manufacturer and supplier of high-quality aluminium finishing chemicals, providing reliable and innovative solutions to the aluminium finishing industry.

What we do

Chemicals from Bangalore, plants from Italy.

In addition to chemicals, we provide engineering solutions and customized plants and equipment from Italy for extrusion die cleaning, anodizing, powder coating, and aluminium surface treatment and finishing.

With a combination of Italian engineering expertise and local technical support, we offer customized solutions to meet the specific production requirements of our customers.

  • Anodizing chemicals
  • Pre-treatment chemicals
  • Anodizing plants
  • Powder coating plants
  • Equipment & technologies
  • Engineering & process solutions
