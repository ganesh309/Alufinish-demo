Pre-treatment chemicals

Degreasing, deoxidizing and conversion coating of aluminium before powder coating — chrome and chrome-free — in three proven process sequences.

Browse the range See process sequences
Product portfolio

Five product groups for the pre-treatment line.

Each group covers one stage of the cycle. Combine them in the sequence that fits your line — acid, short acid or alkaline + acid.

Alkaline degreasing

GROUP 01

Alkaline cleaners that remove oils and workshop soils from extruded aluminium before deoxidizing.

  • Itaclean 113 TDS
  • Itaclean 118 TDS

Acid degreasing

GROUP 02

Acid cleaners for degreasing aluminium in acid pre-treatment cycles, followed by acid deoxidizing.

  • Itadox 161 AB TDS
  • Itaclean IP 4000 TDS

2 in 1

GROUP 03

Combined degreasing and deoxidizing in a single step — the basis of the short acid cycle.

  • Itaclean 211 TDS
  • Itadox 307 NT TDS

Desmutting

GROUP 04

Acid deoxidizing that removes oxides and smut so the conversion coating forms evenly.

  • Desmut A30 TDS
  • Itadox 302 TDS

Chrome & chrome-free conversion

GROUP 05

Conversion coatings — chrome and chrome-free — that give adhesion and corrosion protection under the powder coat.

  • Cover Gold 50R TDS
  • Alugold SCR TDS

Not sure which cycle fits?

Line length, substrate condition and the finish you need decide between the short acid, acid and alkaline + acid sequences. Our technical team will walk through it with you.
